VRHS Hacktive Boba Drops

Here's how to participate in our VRHS Hacktive Boba Drops!

Just follow the instructions below:

  1. Navigate to the Hack Club Boba Drops page.


  2. Open the slideshow and follow along.


  3. Make your own custom website on Github.


  4. Fill out the form to show that you've made a site.


  5. Navigate to the Hack Club Boba Drops page.


  6. Collect your 5 dollar Boba drink next meeting!
